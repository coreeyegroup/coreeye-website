Core Eye Finance

Understand the decision. Structure the intelligence.+
THE RESPONSIBILITY

Understand the decision. Structure the intelligence.

Bring market context, financial information and risk considerations into a clearer framework for discussion and oversight.

WHEN IT BECOMES RELEVANT

Fragmented financial information, inconsistent reporting or a need to understand technology supporting investment operations.

POTENTIAL SCOPE
  • Financial information and reporting requirements
  • Market intelligence and risk-context research
  • Technology requirements for decision-support systems
DEFINED OUTPUT

An intelligence brief, reporting framework or technology specification, as agreed.

The mandate must distinguish research, technology, advisory and execution activities, including any applicable permissions.

02

Core Eye IT

Engineer the foundations that continuity depends on.+
THE RESPONSIBILITY

Engineer the foundations that continuity depends on.

Connect enterprise software, infrastructure and operational processes through maintainable systems with clear ownership.

WHEN IT BECOMES RELEVANT

Disconnected platforms, manual workflows, fragile infrastructure or dependence on individual suppliers.

POTENTIAL SCOPE
  • Systems and dependency mapping
  • Enterprise application and integration design
  • Infrastructure, recovery and ownership planning
DEFINED OUTPUT

An architecture blueprint, prioritised implementation plan, documented system or handover package.

Delivery scope, hosting responsibilities, recovery objectives and ongoing support are agreed for each engagement.

03

Core Eye Cyber

Understand exposure. Prioritise protection.+
THE RESPONSIBILITY

Understand exposure. Prioritise protection.

Assess how identities, information and communications may be exposed, then define proportionate protective measures.

WHEN IT BECOMES RELEVANT

Sensitive information spread across services, exposed principals, impersonation concerns or unclear access controls.

POTENTIAL SCOPE
  • Digital exposure and access assessment
  • Identity and communication safeguards
  • Risk prioritisation and response preparation
DEFINED OUTPUT

An exposure assessment, prioritised protection plan or agreed security implementation.

Assessment and testing require explicit authorisation; protection reduces risk and cannot eliminate every threat.

04

Core Eye Digital

Make public presence worthy of private substance.+
THE RESPONSIBILITY

Make public presence worthy of private substance.

Align identity, websites and digital communications with the institution or principal they represent.

WHEN IT BECOMES RELEVANT

Inconsistent public information, fragmented brand identity, unmanaged visibility or digital assets without clear custodianship.

POTENTIAL SCOPE
  • Institutional identity and website systems
  • Public presence and content governance
  • Reputation awareness and digital legacy planning
DEFINED OUTPUT

An identity framework, website, editorial plan or digital asset stewardship guide.

Public visibility and reputation are influenced by external parties; outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
